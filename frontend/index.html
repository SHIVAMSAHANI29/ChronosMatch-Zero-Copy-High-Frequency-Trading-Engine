About ChronosMatch

ChronosMatch is a trading-engine project with a clean, accessible interface for exploring stock-market workflows. It brings stock discovery, order entry, portfolio views, and copy-trading concepts together while providing a frontend connection to the ChronosMatch API.

This is a demonstration interface, not a live brokerage service or investment recommendation. Prices, wallet balances, portfolio holdings, and trader performance shown here may be simulated. Trading involves risk.
